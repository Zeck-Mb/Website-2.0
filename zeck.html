03 · My journey

The growth happened in small, honest steps.

My theme is active growth. I started with clear interests but also with habits that made progress harder: waiting for others in group discussions, struggling to start schoolwork, and feeling nervous when presenting.

The projects in this portfolio helped me turn those observations into experiments. I learned to begin with the highest-priority task, protect focused study time, and make at least one contribution when working with a group.

  1. Starting point

    Interests without a clear system

    I knew I liked technology, games, and visual work, but I needed stronger routines and more confidence.

  2. Turning point

    Make the next step visible

    Prioritization gave me a clear task to start with. Tracking my habits made progress easier to see honestly.

  3. Practice now

    Use my voice in the group

    I am practicing one idea, one question, or one alternative before a decision is made.

  4. Looking forward

    Build, present, improve

    My next goal is to learn JavaScript, build three simple websites, and explain my work with more confidence.

What comes next

More practice.
More proof.

My career goal is web development, with game design as another creative direction I want to explore. I will keep developing JavaScript, databases, communication, and the confidence to take an idea from a rough beginning to a finished project.

My commitment I will keep showing up for the next small step, even when the result is not perfect yet.
